# AGENTS.md

Public TuxCare documentation (VuePress, `docs/`). Build: `yarn docs:build`.

## Facts

- Publish only what you verified against a source: the code, the repository, the build or release
  system, or the product owner (through the task author).
- A fact you do not know is not written: ask the task author before opening the PR. Never send readers
  to sales or support in its place — they act on it, and the team they reach has no answer. Send
  readers there only for the cases below.

## Where to send readers

- `sales@tuxcare.com` — prospective customers: a feature, version, OS or architecture not yet offered,
  licensing, and access credentials (Nexus logins, registry tokens).
- [TuxCare Support Portal](https://tuxcare.com/support-portal/) — customers using a product they already
  have: signature and key verification, SBOM availability, source archives, errors.
